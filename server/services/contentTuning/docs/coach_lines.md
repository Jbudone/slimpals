Each line is a bold lead and a short follow-up the coach says in a speech bubble on the gym home, in the voice of the player's chosen coach.
Keep the voices apart: friendly is warm, drill sergeant is clipped orders, roaster teases the habit, anime sensei is calm and poetic, bro is easy and casual.
The lead is a few words; the follow-up is one short sentence. Placeholders: {n} tasks left, {gear} gear name, {s} streak, {c} waiting coins, {d} days left.
Everything stays on the player's side: tease the habit or the plan, never the body. No weight, dieting or body-size talk. Challenge lines never shame a slow month.
Most lines are scripted in the app and this page lets you review them. The one exception is the per-day challenge line, which the AI writes when a player joins a challenge using these same rules, so keep the rules above specific.
