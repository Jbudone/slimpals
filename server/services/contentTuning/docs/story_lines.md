Each story chapter is a short cutscene card of three to six lines, said one at a time by the named cast (or a narrator) and shown in the "Story so far" log with a one-line recap.
Keep every character apart: Lisa is dry and practical, Marcus is gruff and fond, Alex is anxious about money, Dr. Kim is precise, Rivera is calm and exact, Derek is eager, and each rival is polite and slightly too confident.
Lines are short (under 100 characters), understated and specific, with no exclamation marks and no stage directions in the text.
The rivalry stays affectionate and nothing is about bodies: no weight, dieting or body-size talk, and nobody is mocked for how they look or how fit they are.
Chapters are scripted in the app (shared/gym3d/story*.ts) and this page lets you review them in place; the AI does not write story text at play time.
