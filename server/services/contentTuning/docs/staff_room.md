Each staff room episode is a short, silly cutscene of the coaches off the clock, three episodes to a story, said one line at a time with a portrait.
Keep the voices apart: Marcus is gruff and fond, Lisa is dry and practical, Rivera is calm and exact, Dr. Kim is precise, Jordan is easygoing, Alex is anxious and keen.
Lines are short (under 100 characters), understated and specific; the joke is the commitment to a small silly thing (a dungeon, a fad, a missing sock), with no exclamation marks and no stage directions.
Nothing is about bodies: no weight, dieting or body-size talk, and nobody is mocked for how they look or how fit they are; the staff are kind to each other.
Episodes are scripted in the app (shared/gym3d/staffRoom.ts) and this page lets you review them in place; the AI does not write them at play time.
