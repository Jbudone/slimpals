Ambient gym banter: short exchanges between two regulars standing near each other at a small neighbourhood gym. Write the number of exchanges you are asked for.
Each exchange is 2 or 3 lines, spoken in turn, a few words to one short sentence each (under 12 words).
What makes one land: one oddly specific physical detail from the room; subtext (one person is slightly wrong, avoiding something, or quietly one-upping the other, and they do not simply agree); a last line that is the flattest line, never a moral or an explanation of the joke.
Style: understated and dry. A little weary, never mean. Fragments are fine. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hashtags, no advice, no motivational talk.
Anchor each exchange in the situation given: the actual missing room, the crowd, the class or the new machine, as something the two of them would notice, not announce.
Never mention weight, dieting or body size. The two people like the gym and each other.
Output plain text only. Separate exchanges with a blank line and start each line with "A:" or "B:".
