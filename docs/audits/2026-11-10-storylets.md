# Audit 8: systemic narrative design, "storylets" (2026-11-10)

From a Fable subagent briefed as a systemic narrative designer (Wildermyth / Hades / quality-based narrative traditions). Inventory of game state is verified for `BanterContext`, `coachLines.ts`, `NpcSimState`, relationships, wallet and `gym_rewards`; check-in, meal, tournament and friend state are inferred from CLAUDE.md.

## Point of view
Stop asking a model to "be funny about the gym". Build one small engine: storylets, short authored scenes that each carry a plain premise, named speakers, a precondition on real game state, and a running-gag counter. The owner supplies one-line premises (2 minutes each); Claude writes six takes; the owner edits and locks one; the engine decides when it plays.
Friday D&D and Bro Hype are two "shows" on the same engine. Why the samples failed: punchlines to setups nobody saw; a catchphrase is funny on its fifth use, not as step one; three 14-word lines cannot hold a premise and a bubble between two passers-by is the worst container; voice cards are not the fix (a tic is not a joke).
Rule: every storylet opens with a narrator line that states the situation so a stranger can picture it; catchphrases are gag steps unlocked only after an earlier step has played for that player.

## Unused game state (biggest assets)
Who is physically present and what they are doing (`NpcSimState`); the player's day (check-in time, meal rating, tasks left, coins idle, days away); friends' activity; memory of what the player has seen (`gym_rewards` claims, a free memory store). Banter today sees only room/gear/crowd/event/season context.

## The system (`shared/gym3d/storylets.ts`, pure; no migration)
`Storylet` = id, show (banter | scene | dnd | brohype), premise (the owner's one line, never shown in game), cast, `when` conditions on a flat `Facts` snapshot (now, player, gym, present, friends, tourney, gags, seen), weight, repeat (never | weekly | daily), lines (line 1 sets the scene; slots {streak} {dish} {friend} {coins}; `alt` fallback), effects (gag counters), status (draft | locked; only locked play).
Selector: filter by status, show, conditions, cast present (banter), repeat rule; score = 2 per condition + 4 if it reacts to a player fact + 3 if it advances a gag + (unseen +3, seen -6) + weight; pick randomly among the top 3. Storage: gag counters and seen marks as `gym_rewards` claims (`gag:<key>:<n>`, `sl:<id>`); locked storylets live in a committed TS file.
Plug-in: banter asks `pick(..., "banter")` first, then falls back to `pickBanter` (bubbles up to 4 lines for storylets); `scene`/`dnd`/`brohype` play in the existing `StaffRoomCard` (`RoomLine` is already `{who, text}`) with a Friday slot for D&D, a Sunday slot for Bro Hype and an "Overheard" card for state-triggered scenes (at most one a day); the story card stays as is. A seed in the tools engineer's page is exactly premise + cast + when.
Who writes what: owner = a one-sentence premise (5 in 2 minutes), picking and editing a take (~90 s), saying "again" or "dead" to a gag; Claude = proposes cast and `when`, writes six takes that differ in angle, proposes gag steps and `alt` lines, wires everything. AI prompt rules: line 1 is pictureable narration; one odd thing, everyone else reacts sanely; end on a reaction; no catchphrase unless its setup has played. Nothing plays without the owner's lock (volume ~60 a month).

## Build plan
Day 1 (Claude, about a day): `storylets.ts` with tests, a Facts builder, the 12 drafts, play through `StaffRoomCard` with a Friday slot and "Overheard" trigger, add speakers (Tom, Elena, Priya, Rex, Brody, Chad, Kyle) with flat faces, an inbox card "Read 12 scenes, lock or kill". Week 1: the seed page, edit pairs fed back, banter storylets in bubbles, gag counters, friend names in Facts, owner seeds 15 premises. Month 1: a D&D season of 8 driven by group check-ins, a Bro Hype ladder of 6 fads with Brody visibly in the gym on Sundays, a "that was funny" tap, friend doubles as cast.
Freeze: nightly banter job and judge, bake-off, writers' room, story council, campaigns 5-12, daily report. Keep: budgeted `modelClient` and spend ledger. Retire once covered: the anonymous banter pool except missing-room lines. Delete: "no exclamation marks" and "dry" style rules.
Test (owner 15 minutes): read the 12, mark each lock/edit/kill, type 5 premises. Worked if at least 6 of 12 lock with light edits (fewer than 4 = premise-first writing by Claude is not good enough; the owner writes lines and the AI only formats), a state-triggered scene fires at the right moment and makes the owner smile because it was about them, and at least one take per owner premise is lockable.
